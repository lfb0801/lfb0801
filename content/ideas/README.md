This directory is a digital garden rather than a publication schedule. Notes can begin as a question, collect counterexamples, and mature into an essay or project.

## What belongs here

- design principles I want to pressure-test
- patterns that connect code, delivery, and team learning
- tools that make hidden engineering work inspectable
- arguments I am not yet certain enough to present as conclusions

The status of a note should be honest. “Seed” is useful information, not an apology.
