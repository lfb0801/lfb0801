# About Lloyd

I’m a software engineer interested in the parts of engineering that are easy to lose: why a design was chosen, what trade-off shaped a pipeline, which constraint made a refactor worthwhile, and how a team can carry that reasoning forward.

## What I keep returning to

- **Java and backend design** — creating systems whose boundaries explain themselves
- **GitLab, CI/CD, and delivery** — making the route from idea to production observable
- **Refactoring and engineering education** — turning tacit judgment into something teachable
- **AI-assisted engineering** — using agents to sharpen decisions without outsourcing responsibility

I leave career labels out of this garden. The work and the quality of the reasoning should carry that signal themselves.

## Why a terminal?

A terminal rewards curiosity. It exposes structure without pretending every visitor must follow the same polished funnel. You can read the default file and leave, or explore until you find the rough edges.

This interface is theatrical. Its boundaries are not: commands are allowlisted, files are read-only, and no input is ever evaluated as code.
